Our Story

Brewing happiness since 2015.

Founded in 2015, BTech Cafe Walla began with a simple dream: to create a space where people could come together over exceptional coffee and delicious food. What started as a small corner shop has grown into a beloved destination for coffee lovers across the city.

Our team of award-winning baristas travels the world to source the finest single-origin beans from Colombia, Ethiopia and Brazil. Every cup is a testament to our unwavering commitment to quality, sustainability and community.

Today, with over 50,000 happy customers served and a 4.9-star rating, we continue to push the boundaries of what a cafe can be. Join us and become part of our story!
