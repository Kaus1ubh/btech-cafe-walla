About the café

BTech Cafe Walla is a small café for students. The menu is short on purpose: chai, coffee, Maggi, sandwiches and a couple of sweet things, all priced for a hostel budget.

You can sit with a laptop for as long as you like. There are plug points along the wall, and nobody will ask you to order a second chai.

If you're short on time between lectures, order on this site, then pick it up at the counter and pay there.
